Welcome to falco-ctf challenge 01: read-shadow
================================================

Goal:
  Get the Falco rule "Read sensitive file untrusted" to fire from this container.

Hint:
  /etc/shadow stores hashed passwords on Linux. It's normally readable
  only by root — but root inside this container is unprivileged from the
  host's view. Try opening it.

When you succeed, the scoreboard at http://scoreboard.192.168.64.2.nip.io/
will tick this challenge as solved for your user.
